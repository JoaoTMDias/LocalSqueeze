# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not share files or personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially when possible.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
